Discover Festifood: A Culinary Festival in Mons

Festifood promises to be a celebration of flavors and local artisans. It's the perfect moment to appreciate Belgian gastronomy while soaking in the warm atmosphere of Mons, echoing the spirit of Dinédit.

Date de l'événement September 10, 2026
Région Mons
Publié le September 3, 2026
Lecture 2 min

From September 10 to 13, 2026, Mons will host Festifood, a festival dedicated to gastronomy that highlights local productions and talents from the region. This event promises to be a true delight for the taste buds, where visitors can savor dishes carefully prepared by passionate artisans.

At Dinédit, we are always on the lookout for moments that celebrate gastronomy in unique places, and Festifood is no exception. This event resonates with our approach that combines culinary art and unexpected discoveries. Meanwhile, we invite you to explore our unique dinners, where each encounter is a complete sensory experience. Discover our upcoming unique dinners and let yourself be surprised by our offerings.

To get to Festifood, head to Mons, where the vibrant and friendly atmosphere will be waiting for you. Mark your calendars: from September 10 to 13, 2026. For more information, feel free to check Dinédit — Meeting Alchemy or ask Anaïs for more details. visitmons.be
