A Sensory Walk in Liège: From Naimette to Bernalmont

On August 16, 2026, embark on a journey through the parks and hills of Liège. A perfect opportunity to savor Dinédit's gastronomy in an inspiring setting.

Date de l'événement August 16, 2026
Région Liège
Publié le July 1, 2026
Lecture 2 min

We have identified a captivating guided tour that takes you from Naimette to Bernalmont, through the lush landscapes of Liège. This route highlights the natural and historical riches of the region, offering an immersive experience that resonates with our passion for art and gastronomy.

At Dinédit, we believe that every moment spent exploring a place should be accompanied by a memorable gastronomic experience. This Sunday, August 16, 2026, let yourself be carried away by the stories of the city while looking forward to a dinner that could extend this sensory adventure. Discover our upcoming unique dinners that could perfectly complement this moment of discovery.

The tour begins in Naimette and unfolds in a picturesque setting, perfect for those who enjoy a stroll in the open air. For more information about this stage of the Grand Tour of Liège, check the source visitezliege.be. And if you wish to explore how to integrate this experience with a dinner, feel free to contact Anaïs.
