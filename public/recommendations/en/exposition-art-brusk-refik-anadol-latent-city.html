A Sensory Immersion with Refik Anadol in Bruges

Refik Anadol's exhibition at BRUSK transports us into a fascinating visual universe where art and technology merge. At Dinédit, we love exploring these experiences that awaken the senses.

Date de l'événement May 8, 2026
Région Bruges
Publié le July 13, 2026
Lecture 2 min

We have spotted the captivating exhibition by Refik Anadol, titled "Latent City," which will take place at BRUSK starting May 8, 2026. This renowned artist, a pioneer of new media, offers an immersive experience where data and light meet to create a striking artistic dialogue.

At Dinédit, we believe that gastronomy and art can intertwine to enrich our shared moments. A visit to this exhibition could perfectly align with one of our unique dinners, providing a harmony between flavors and visual emotions. You can discover our upcoming unique dinners to extend this experience.

The exhibition will be held in Bruges, a place at the crossroads of history and innovation. To access it, follow the directions on the BRUSK website. For any questions, feel free to interact with Anaïs who can provide you with information. visitbruges.be
